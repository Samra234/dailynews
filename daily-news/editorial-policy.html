Editorial & Fact-Checking Policy

International Journalism Standards • Sourcing Protocol • Copyright Compliance

The editorial mission of Daily News is to provide clear, reliable, and verified global news reporting. We believe trust is earned through factual accuracy, transparent sourcing, independence, and swift accountability.

1. Multi-Tiered Verification & Sourcing

Daily News adheres to standard wire-service verification protocols before publishing headlines or reporting briefs:

  • Primary Documentation: Wherever feasible, stories rely on primary sources, official government press communiqués, legal filings, and direct corporate announcements.
  • Dual-Source Corroboration: Breaking or contentious geopolitical reports require corroboration from at least two reputable, independent news agencies before inclusion in our featured sections.
  • Attribution: We never present outside reportage as our own. Each article card clearly features the reporting publisher's name and provides an outbound link directly to the primary publisher's site.

2. Fact-Checking Guidelines

Our editorial desk actively investigates claims made in public discourse. When verifying statements by public officials or viral claims:

  • We cross-reference historical statistical databases, official gazettes, and peer-reviewed studies.
  • We clearly differentiate between factual claims, analytical projections, and opinion commentary.
  • We do not rely on single anonymous social media posts as verified breaking news.

3. Corrections Policy

Despite meticulous efforts, errors may occasionally occur. Daily News handles corrections swiftly, visibly, and without evasion:

  • Minor Errors (Typographical / Grammatical): Fixed promptly upon identification.
  • Substantive Factual Errors: A prominent correction note is appended indicating what was originally reported, what the verified facts are, and the exact timestamp when the correction was made.
  • Retractions: If a report is found to be fundamentally incorrect, it is withdrawn with a clear explanatory notice explaining the circumstances.

4. Copyright, Fair Use & DMCA Compliance

Daily News operates strictly under the principles of fair use for digital news aggregation and commentary. We display permitted headlines, brief descriptive excerpts, and thumbnails licensed via news APIs or fair use doctrine, always directing visitors to the original publisher for full long-form articles.

If you are a copyright owner and believe your copyrighted work has been utilized in a manner that constitutes infringement, please submit a notice to our designated agent at dmca@dailynews.portal with full source documentation.

5. Non-Partisanship & Conflict of Interest

Daily News maintains a policy of editorial neutrality. Our staff writers and curation editors are forbidden from holding financial stakes in companies they cover or participating in partisan political activism that could compromise the impartial presentation of news.
