About Daily News

Published by Daily News Media Group • Last Revised: October 2026

Our Mission

Founded with the conviction that credible, timely journalism is the foundation of an informed society, Daily News is an independent digital news portal that curates, synthesizes, and highlights verified stories from across the globe. Our platform brings together real-time reporting on international affairs, regional politics with in-depth coverage of Pakistan, technology breakthroughs, financial markets, sports, health, and science.

Journalistic Integrity & Transparency

In an era of rampant misinformation and synthetic media, Daily News operates on the core values of factual precision, verified sourcing, and fair attribution. We do not manufacture clickbait or publish uncorroborated rumors. Every story displayed on our platform is retrieved via authenticated journalistic wire APIs, direct publisher feeds, and established newsrooms.

When our editors provide analysis or summaries, we clearly attribute the primary investigative work to the originating news agency (such as Reuters, BBC, Associated Press, Dawn, Bloomberg, and other international outlets) and provide direct links enabling readers to review the full original article.

Advertising & Commercial Independence

Daily News is supported by digital advertising, including Google AdSense and programmatic network placements. To maintain absolute journalistic credibility, we strictly enforce a separation between editorial operations and commercial sponsorships.

  • No advertiser or sponsor exercises editorial influence over our coverage, headline selection, or story curation.
  • All commercial and sponsored slots are explicitly marked with an "Advertisement" or "Sponsored" label in compliance with Google AdSense guidelines and FTC disclosure rules.
  • We do not accept deceptive advertorials or concealed native paid articles.

Corrections & Feedback Policy

When a factual error is discovered, our policy is to promptly and transparently correct it. Readers who identify an inaccuracy are encouraged to notify our corrections desk at corrections@dailynews.portal.
